## Implemented qualification

Added `oracle_factorization.py` and `run_p13.py` for the fixed-pair predicate
oracle, legal Top-L controls, exact additive residual checks, and A/B/C/D
decomposition.  Added contract tests in
`models/relation_decision_regret_v1/tests/test_oracle_factorization.py`.

Commands used:

```text
python -m pytest -q models/relation_decision_regret_v1/tests
python models/relation_decision_regret_v1/run_p13.py ... --output results/relation_decision_regret_v1/p1_3/full_test.json --bootstrap-replicates 100
```

The full-test artifact has 2,177 relation-bearing official-test entries and 52
missing carrier predictions.  At K=50/L=3, fixed-pair predicate arbitration
recovers 12.51 percentage points of mR (95% grouped bootstrap CI
11.40--14.61), while the post-predicate slot headroom is 1.19 pp and the
factorization coupling is +0.40 pp.  The additivity residual is exactly zero
in the legal swap trials.  Therefore the set-dependent utility/swap mechanism
is closed; predicate arbitration is the dominant actionable problem, with a
small residual coupling worth testing only after HPA.

The artifact's L0--L4 entries are fit-free protocol proxies.  They verify that
the decoder can expose and legally collapse Top-L hypotheses, but they do not
implement the plan's fit-trained bias, temperature, and multinomial controls.
Consequently the formal simple-control saturation gate remains pending.  The
fit500 to dev500 CPU pilot (`run_mpu_hpa_pilot.py`) is positive in R, but is
explicitly not an mR gate because neither 500-image subset covers all 56
predicates.  Formal learner promotion requires a full hidden carrier export,
fit-trained L0--L4 controls, and a clean fit/dev mR evaluation.
