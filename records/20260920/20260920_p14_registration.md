# P1.4 registration

The next experiment is registered as `experiments/relation_decision_regret_v1/P14_FULL_DEV_GATE.json`.
It follows the p1.3 finding that fixed-pair Top-3 predicate arbitration is the
primary headroom and keeps confirm locked.  The new carrier export is sharded
at 500 images, compacts each raw inference pickle to Top-50 physical support,
and stores raw files only transiently in the external registered cache.

No external download is authorized.  The existing epoch-28 DSFormer
checkpoint, OpenPSG annotation, panoptic GT, and v2 grouped fit/dev manifest
are the only inputs.  Full GPU export is intentionally not started in this
registration step; use `export_p14_carrier.sh` after checking free GPU and
disk capacity.

## Export smoke

The first fit shard (`fit_000`, 500 images) was exported on GPU 6 with
`--export-pair-features`.  It completed with 500/500 images and zero missing
predictions.  The raw all-pairs pickle was compacted to the registered
Top-50-support carrier:

```text

sha256: cd31684c6cd61662bff4c085af9eaeeb95b23f48f22e34d90cc83e2c7dc5817a
```

The raw shard was removed after compaction.  The compact carrier contains
float32 predicate scores and float16 pair features, with masks and bounding
boxes omitted because they are rebuildable from the registered raw carrier.
The full fit/dev plan is 66 + 14 shards; no confirm shard is generated by the
export script.

The second fit smoke (`fit_001`, GPU 7) completed in about seven minutes and
produced a 25.2 MB compact carrier.  Its initial five missing rows were
relation-free images for which Fair PSG emits no relation carrier; the compact
tool now excludes such rows from the missing-prediction count while retaining
them as empty image records.

The two smoke shards merge cleanly to 1,000 unique image IDs and 56 predicate
classes.  The second artifact currently retains the pre-fix metadata list for
those five relation-free rows; this does not affect its empty carrier records
or the merge uniqueness check.  Future shards use the corrected counter.
